Backup: SWOT for GreenPow in Estonian MedTech

Strengths

  • Orchestration engine already built and tested
  • Technology agnostic: plugs into existing infrastructure
  • Coretex audit trail for GDPR and MDR files
  • No hardware to buy, so little capital needed

Weaknesses

  • Certifications not yet confirmed
  • Supply depends on partners' idle hours
  • Estonian capacity not yet confirmed
  • Thin margin per hour ($0.40), so volume matters

Opportunities

  • AI Act and MDR push health AI toward auditable compute
  • EU health data space: secure environments from March 2029
  • Sovereignty concerns about US-parented clouds
  • Expansion to the Nordics and Baltics

Threats

  • Subsidised HPC and LUMI for researchers
  • Partners may refuse external jobs
  • Marketplaces or sovereign clouds could add scheduling
  • Small, grant-funded startup budgets
